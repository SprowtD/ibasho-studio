Services

Keys stay on the computer that runs Ibasho, in saved/connections.json, readable only by its owner. They are never shown again after you save them (only the last four characters), never put in git, and never sent to anyone but the service they belong to. Anyone who can open Ibasho on your network can use your connections, so only share its address with people you trust.
